# Selected work notes

I build applications around the everyday work of planning, reviewing, and keeping information current. These are high-level summaries of contributions to internal products, with company-specific details omitted.

## Staff scheduling

Scheduling involves more than assigning a shift. Availability, change requests, approvals, and the published schedule need to stay connected.

My contributions include workflows for availability and assignments, shift changes with clear approval states, and schedule views appropriate to each person's role. I also worked on publishing safeguards, reports, and communication views.

The design focus is making the state of a change clear: what has been requested, what has been reviewed, and what people should act on.

## Recruiting workflows

A recruiting workspace needs to preserve a person's history while keeping individual applications easy to find and review.

I contributed to a workspace connecting searchable profiles, applications, team notes, and saved documents. I also improved duplicate-profile merging so existing applications and documents stay attached to the resulting profile.

The engineering focus is continuity: cleaning up a record should preserve the context people have already collected.

## Event coordination

Planning an appearance or event requires people information, scheduling, and logistics to remain connected.

I built out a workspace combining participant profiles, appearance schedules, and logistics tracking, with protected access and tools for keeping information current.

The design focus is helping people move between the participant and the plan without losing the surrounding context.

## Dependable integrations

An import should be something people can review and trust. A refresh should also handle a source becoming unavailable.

My contributions include draft imports, duplicate review, safeguards that preserve staff edits, and refresh behavior that keeps saved results available during source outages. I also worked on migration checks that verify existing records and files.

The engineering focus is protecting work already done while making incoming changes easier to inspect.
